Karl Ebeneser Karlsson

1950 – 2024

Some people belong to a particular time in our lives, but never quite leave us.

I first met Karl many years ago, when our lives crossed paths. We spent a great deal of time together in those years, and I came to know a man who could be stubborn, funny, generous and fiercely loyal.

Karl had a rare ability to make people feel seen. He understood that sometimes what a person needs most is simply someone willing to listen and be there.

We shared countless cups of coffee, long conversations and occasional disagreements. Karl could be stubborn. Those who knew him well would smile at that. But his stubbornness came from conviction. He believed deeply in second chances. In later years, life took us in different directions. We did not see each other for a long time, but I often thought of him and of the years we shared.

Karl was a complicated man. But I will remember the good he tried to do. And I will remember the things we carried together.

Rest in peace, old friend.

Halldóra
