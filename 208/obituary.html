Karl Ebeneser Karlsson

1950 – 2024

Karl Ebeneser Karlsson passed away peacefully surrounded by his loving family. He was a devoted father, a caring husband, and a pillar of his local community. Known for his generous spirit and unwavering kindness, Karl touched the lives of everyone who had the privilege of knowing him.

Born in 1950, Karl grew up with a deep appreciation for the outdoors and mechanics. He spent his career working diligently to provide for his family, always putting their needs ahead of his own. In his free time, he could often be found in his garage, restoring classic cars, or sharing stories of his youth with his grandchildren.

He is survived by his beloved wife of 50 years, his three children, and six grandchildren. A private memorial service will be held for close family and friends. In lieu of flowers, the family requests that donations be made to his favorite local charity.
