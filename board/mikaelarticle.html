Mikael Arason out after car crash

National team midfielder to miss next three club fixtures following a reported single-vehicle accident in heavy rain on Sunday night.

By Gunnar Steinn Pálsson
Published: 27 July 2026, 15:20 • Updated: 27/08/2026, 19:14

National team midfielder Mikael Arason will miss his club's next three games after a car accident on his way home from training on Sunday night.

According to a short statement from the club, Mikael lost control of his car in heavy rain and went off the road. He was treated for a broken nose and two broken fingers on his left hand, and lost several teeth. No other vehicle was involved, and police were not called to the scene.

"I'm lucky it wasn't worse. Thanks for all the kind messages. I'll be back soon."

— Mikael Arason, writing in a brief message on Instagram

Mikael will miss league games against [Club A], [Club B] and [Club C]. The club expects him back before Iceland's qualifiers in September. The national team coach said he was "relieved it wasn't more serious".

The club would not answer further questions about the accident.
